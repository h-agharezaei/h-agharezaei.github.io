+++
title = "Cold wallet"
summary = "A wallet whose signing keys stay offline, holding most funds and signing rarely, usually with manual approval."
category = "Blockchain"
related = ["hot-wallet", "sweeping", "multisig"]
posts = ["bc-exchange-deposits-withdrawals"]
+++

It is the counterpart of the [hot wallet](/glossary/hot-wallet/): slow to use, but an online attacker cannot ask it to sign. Funds move from cold to hot in planned top-ups, so a hot-wallet compromise only reaches what was kept online.
