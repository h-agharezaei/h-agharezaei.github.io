Each entry looks like `CVE-YEAR-NUMBER` and links to a description, affected versions and references. Severity is usually scored with CVSS from 0 to 10; 9.0 and above is *critical*. A CVE says a flaw exists, not whether you are exploitable, so always check your version and configuration.
