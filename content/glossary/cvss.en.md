The score comes from a vector such as `AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:R/S:U/C:H/I:N/A:N`, which says how the flaw is reached (network, local), how hard it is, whether a user must act, and what it breaks (confidentiality, integrity, availability). In version 3.1, 9.0 and above is Critical, 7.0-8.9 High, 4.0-6.9 Medium. A low score with active exploitation can still be urgent; see [KEV](/glossary/kev/).
