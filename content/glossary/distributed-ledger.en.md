Unlike a replicated database run by one company, the copies belong to parties who may not trust each other. They need a rule ([consensus](/glossary/consensus/)) to agree on the next entry, and a [hash chain](/glossary/hash-chain/) so that rewriting history is visible. A blockchain is one way to build it.
