Solving a problem by combining answers to smaller subproblems, each solved once and stored.

A problem fits when it has [optimal substructure](/glossary/optimal-substructure/) and [overlapping subproblems](/glossary/overlapping-subproblems/). You define a state, a transition, base cases and an order, then fill a table or memoize a recursion.
