+++
title = "Equivocation"
summary = "A validator signing two conflicting messages, such as votes for two different blocks at the same height and round."
category = "Blockchain"
related = ["slashing", "validator", "byzantine-fault"]
posts = ["bc-proof-of-stake-bft"]
+++

Honest validators never do it, so the two signed messages are a self-contained proof that anyone with the public key can check. That proof is the evidence used for [slashing](/glossary/slashing/).
