title = "Liquidity pool"
summary = "A contract holding reserves of two or more tokens that traders swap against; depositors own it through shares."
category = "Blockchain"
related = ["amm", "constant-product"]
posts = ["bc-amm-constant-product"]
+++

A depositor adds both tokens at the current ratio and receives LP shares; burning them returns a proportional part of the reserves, fees included. Pool depth decides how much a trade moves the price.
