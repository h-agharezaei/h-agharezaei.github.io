+++
title = "Little's law"
summary = "In a stable system, average WIP equals throughput times average cycle time."
category = "Engineering"
related = ["wip-limit", "queue"]
posts = ["eng-estimation-planning", "ds-capacity-estimation", "ds-case-studies"]
+++

If a team finishes 0.6 items a day and each takes 5 days, about 3 items are in progress. To cut cycle time without losing throughput, cut work in progress.
