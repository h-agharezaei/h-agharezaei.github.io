The same swap can pay very different amounts depending on what runs before it. Some MEV hurts users, like a [sandwich attack](/glossary/sandwich-attack/); some keeps markets healthy, like [arbitrage](/glossary/arbitrage/) and liquidations. Originally "miner extractable value".
