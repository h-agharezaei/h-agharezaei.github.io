title = "Overcollateralization"
summary = "Lending only against collateral worth more than the loan, so the lender can sell it if the borrower does not repay."

On-chain lending has no identity or courts, so the collateral is the only guarantee. A collateral factor, for example 75%, caps the loan below the collateral value; [liquidation](/glossary/liquidation/) sells collateral when that margin runs out.
