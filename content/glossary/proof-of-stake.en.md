summary = "A consensus approach where voting weight comes from locked stake instead of computation, and provable cheating costs part of that stake."
category = "Blockchain"

Like [proof of work](/glossary/proof-of-work/) it ties influence to a scarce resource, so fake identities add nothing. Instead of burning energy, a [validator](/glossary/validator/) puts money at risk; [slashing](/glossary/slashing/) takes part of it when the validator provably misbehaves.
