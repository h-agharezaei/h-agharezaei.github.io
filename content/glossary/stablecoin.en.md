A stablecoin is a token designed to hold a fixed value, usually one US dollar. Centralized issuers such as Tether (USDT) and Circle (USDC) can freeze tokens held by an address. That is why attackers try to swap stolen stablecoins into assets like ETH or BTC quickly, before a freeze lands.
