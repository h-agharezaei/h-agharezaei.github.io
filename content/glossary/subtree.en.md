+++
title = "Subtree"
summary = "A way to copy another repository's files into a subdirectory of yours, keeping their history mergeable."
category = "Git"
related = ["submodule"]
posts = ["git-submodule-subtree-worktree"]
+++

With `git subtree add/pull/split` the files become ordinary files in your repo, so collaborators need no extra step. `split` can extract a subdirectory's history back out.
