Technical debt is a metaphor: a shortcut gets you something now, and you pay "interest" later as slower and riskier changes. The metaphor is useful only if you can name the cost of leaving it and the cost of fixing it. See [refactoring](/glossary/refactoring/) for one way to pay it down.
