## Welcome

This is my personal blog, and this is its first post. Here I write about things I have learned or worked with, plainly and with examples, so they may be useful to someone else too.

## Why did I create this site?

My reasons are simple:

- I want to share my experience and knowledge with others.
- I want to keep learning.
- I want to connect with people who are interested in the same topics.

## Who is this blog for?

It is for programmers. If you write code and want to understand what happens under the tools you use every day, you are in the right place. The series that introduce a new field, such as blockchain or networking, start from the ground up and do not assume you already know that field.

## What will you read?

Each post starts from a concrete problem or question, states the main idea plainly, and explains every new term where it first appears. I ran the code, and the outputs come from those runs; where I did not run something, the post says so. Words that need an explanation are also in the [glossary](/glossary/).

The topics are grouped into a few series:

- **Languages:** Go and Rust, from a first program to a complete service, plus Node.js and Python. Two more series sit next to these: one on NATS JetStream (messaging with Go) and one called Go + Rust, which puts the two languages side by side.
- **Software engineering foundations:** algorithms, Git, databases, networking, Linux, distributed systems and software engineering.
- **Security:** software security, and now and then a short note on a security incident or vulnerability.
- **Blockchain**, seen from a programmer's point of view.

Every post is available in both Persian and English.

## Where should I start?

Each series has its own page in the [series list](/series/), listing its posts. Pick one that is useful to you and go from its first post. You can also browse everything on the [posts page](/posts/).
