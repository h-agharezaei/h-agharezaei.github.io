A selection of public projects. Many of them are hands-on practice for concepts I wanted to understand deeply.

## [quant-core](https://github.com/h-agharezaei/quant-core)
**Rust** — A high-performance market analysis engine for price action detection, fractal pattern recognition and multi-timeframe market structure. Currently focused on XAUUSD and EURUSD, designed to support any instrument.

## [go-gold-analyzer](https://github.com/h-agharezaei/go-gold-analyzer)
**Go** — Automated technical analysis for gold futures. Pulls Yahoo Finance data every minute, computes RSI, MACD and ATR, and emits BUY / SELL / HOLD signals with file logging.

## [go-nats-demo](https://github.com/h-agharezaei/go-nats-demo)
**Go** — Publisher and worker services on NATS JetStream with durable subscriptions, connection retry and the management dashboard, all via Docker.

## [go-kafka-first](https://github.com/h-agharezaei/go-kafka-first)
**Go** — Hands-on Kafka practice: topics, partitions, offsets and consumer groups, running on KRaft with Kafka UI via Docker Compose.

## [nestjs-structure](https://github.com/h-agharezaei/nestjs-structure)
**TypeScript** — A NestJS project structure with environment-based configuration (default, development, production, test).

## Go concurrency notes
Small experiments on goroutines and `context`: [go-goroutine-01](https://github.com/h-agharezaei/go-goroutine-01), [go-context-01](https://github.com/h-agharezaei/go-context-01), [go-connectivity-test](https://github.com/h-agharezaei/go-connectivity-test).
